About Me

I am an Applied Scientist at Amazon Prime Video. I earned my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Penn State University (2022–2026), advised by Prof. Abhinav Verma, and my M.S. (2022) and B.S. (2019) from USTB, advised by Prof. Xu-Cheng Yin.

My current research centers on Causal World Modeling, MLLM Agent Post-Training & Self-Evolution, and also Judge.

After nearly a decade in computer vision, I have grown most curious about a different question: how agents can model open-ended, creative tasks, capturing human taste and, ultimately, modeling how human society itself evolves. I believe the next frontier is not perceiving the world more accurately, but reasoning about what people find valuable and worth creating.
